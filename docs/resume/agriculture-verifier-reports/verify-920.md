## 2. Independent check of the main figure (7% with bank credit; about 23% in arrears)

- **IFAD Country Strategy and Programme Evaluation, 2019 (EB 2019/126/R.11, para 56):** repeats "no more than 7% in 2012" and "about 120,000" indebted smallholders. Its footnote 49 cites the FAO Investment Centre's 2013 study of agricultural finance in Tunisia, the same WB/FAO/AFD work as SRC-925. **This is repetition, not confirmation.** SRC-926 (2017) also repeats the 7%.
- **WebManagerCenter, 3 Nov 2015 (UTAP seminar, tier C):** banks' share of agricultural investment fell from 15% in the 1990s to 7% in 2014, and only 6% of farmers are insured. This is a different measure. It points the same way (very low formal finance) but gives a lower bank share than SRC-925's 11%.
- **Tunisie Numérique, 12 Jan 2026, quoting the BCT (tier C reporting an official statement):** 33,535 agricultural actors in categories 4-5 at public banks (June 2025), and about TND 4.6 billion of outstanding agricultural credit (Nov 2025). This is the only non-circular data point on arrears.
  - It shows the high-risk stock is far below the about 120,000 indebted farmers of around 2011.
  - Coverage differs (public banks only, categories 4-5 only), so the two numbers are not directly comparable.
- **Conclusion:** the 7% figure was never independently remeasured. It rests on one 2012 study. The record already says so in `data_gaps`, which is correct.

## 3. Tiers, taxonomy, timeframe, root causes, confidence

- **Tiers:**
  - The World Bank, WTO, AICS and IFAD reports are rated B, which is correct. The press articles are rated C, which is correct.
  - SRC-922 is rated A. Économie rurale is a peer-reviewed journal, though this is a conference-session paper; A is acceptable.
  - SRC-927 and SRC-932 are typed `official-statistics`, but they are legal texts. The template has no `legislation` type; propose adding one to the template, or note it. Tier A for an official gazette is defensible.
- **Frontmatter:**
  - All fields match `templates/finding.md`.
  - Theme, timeframe, geography and root-cause types are all valid taxonomy values.
  - All `related` IDs exist, including FND-TG-881.
  - FND-781 does say the title-to-credit link is asserted, not tested, which matches how FND-922 cites it.
- **Timeframe:** FND-920 as `past` (1980-1995, played out by about 1995, effects carried into FND-921) is correct. FND-921 and FND-922 as `current` are fine, but FND-922's core numbers date from around 2011.
- **Root causes:** these are causes, not symptoms. FND-922 explicitly separates the two. Two classification remarks:
  - FND-922 root cause 4 is typed `climatic`. The cause as written is the missing drought insurance, which is institutional or market; the climate is the hazard behind it. Consider `institutional`, or split it into two causes.
  - FND-922 root cause 2 rests partly on tier C for the 2014 law. See fix 922-b.
- **Confidence:** `medium` is justified for all three.
  - FND-920 and FND-921 rest on lender self-evaluations and models, and the records say so.
  - In FND-922, the tier-C parts are flagged in the indicators and in "Why confidence". The Finance Law 2014 sentence in root cause 2 is not flagged.
