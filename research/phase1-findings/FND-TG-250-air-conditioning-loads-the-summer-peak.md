## Problem

Air conditioning is a material contributor to Tunisia's summer electricity peak. LBNL reports
that STEG recorded a 4,472 MW peak in August 2021 and estimated that air conditioning accounted
for 44% of it. The 2019 household survey found 47.9% AC ownership, a stock of 1.681 million units,
and 757.3 GWh annual stock consumption (SRC-TG-250). These figures show a demand-side peak driver;
the underlying STEG survey and peak attribution were not independently available.

## Root causes

Rising household ownership increased the installed AC stock and its simultaneous summer demand.
The cited source documents the trend but does not separate income, housing, weather, and equipment
efficiency as causal drivers.

## Evidence

STEG survey values and peak estimate as reported in LBNL's May 2023 market assessment
(SRC-TG-250); companion load modelling in SRC-TG-251 uses assumptions for coincidence.

## Why confidence is what it is

Medium: the institutional report gives a clear survey series, but its primary STEG survey reports
were not located, and a separate 2013 STEG peak share in SRC-TG-251 is not comparable to the 2021
44% estimate.

## Links to other sectors

Cooling demand is associated with building comfort and household electricity expenditure; the
sources do not quantify distribution by income.

## Leads for phase 2
