## Problem

The World Bank's static CBAM exposure analysis includes Tunisia among the ten selected cement
exporters with higher trade exposure (SRC-TG-292). The estimate uses 2022 mirror trade data and benchmarked emissions,
with a notional carbon price; it is not a forecast of firm payments. EU Regulation 2023/956
establishes the CBAM framework (SRC-TG-294).

## Root causes

The model combines cement carbon intensity relative to EU production with the share and value of
exports to the EU. It does not provide firm-level emissions or actual payment data.

## Evidence

Method and chart are in SRC-TG-292; EU regulation is in SRC-TG-294.

## Why confidence is what it is

Medium for the existence and relative ranking of modelled exposure; the report gives no exact
Tunisian value and the index is not a realized cost.

## Links to other sectors

CBAM is an EU trade and climate policy affecting an energy-intensive industrial subsector.

## Leads for phase 2

- EU CBAM Regulation (SRC-TG-294).
