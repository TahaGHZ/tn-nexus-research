## Extracts

Printed p. 14 (PDF p. 15), "Gas Pipelines from Algeria to Europe":

> The Trans-Mediterranean Pipeline (TransMed) is a natural gas pipeline running from Algeria via Tunisia to Sicily and Italy, constructed in 1978-1983 and 1991-1994.

English gloss: TransMed runs from Algeria through Tunisia to Sicily and Italy. It was built in
1978-1983 and expanded in 1991-1994.

Printed p. 16 (PDF p. 17), Tunisia section:

> In lieu of transit fees, Tunisia receives natural gas as a royalty from the Trans-Mediterranean Pipeline, which transports natural gas from Algeria to Italy, passing through Tunisian territory.

English gloss: instead of transit fees, Tunisia receives natural gas from the pipeline as a
royalty.

Printed p. 26 (PDF p. 27):

> British Gas (BG) is the largest gas producer in the country and supplies approximately 60% of Tunisia's domestic gas production through the Miskar and Hasdrubal operations. BG Tunisia holds a 100% interest in the Miskar gas field (the most productive in the country), which is 125 km offshore in the Gulf of Gabes. Gas is processed at the Hannibal plant and supplied, under a long-term contract, to Société Tunisienne de l'Électricité et du Gaz (STEG), Tunisia's state electricity and gas company, at international market values and in hard currency.

English gloss: British Gas supplied about 60% of domestic gas production from the Miskar and
Hasdrubal fields. Miskar, 100% BG-owned and 125 km offshore in the Gulf of Gabes, was the most
productive field. Its gas goes to STEG under a long-term contract at international prices and in
hard currency.

## Notes

Advocacy NGO publication, so its framing is not neutral. It is the only source opened that gives
the TransMed construction dates and the Miskar contract terms. The facts are described as current
in 2016; BG was later acquired by Shell, and current ownership was not checked. The report gives
no start date for Miskar production and no royalty rate. Some figures are drawn from EIA. The
text layer was extracted locally from the downloaded PDF.
