## Extracts

Article of 20 July 2026, 15:01, reporting Mounir Dridi, regional director of water operation and distribution for Greater Tunis at SONEDE, on Radio nationale.

> entre 700 et mille interruptions quotidiennes de l'alimentation électrique

> jusqu'à mille interruptions quotidiennes de courant ont été enregistrées

Both for 14 and 15 July 2026, according to the article. A power cut, according to the speaker, "entraîne automatiquement l'arrêt des équipements". The page also says (paraphrased, not quoted): when cuts repeat, reservoirs empty and air enters the pipes; restoring normal supply after power returns can take five to seven hours, depending on network length and reservoir levels.

English gloss: between 700 and 1,000 daily interruptions of the electricity supply (14 and 15 July 2026); a cut automatically stops the equipment.

## Notes

One SONEDE official's statement, press-reported. The count refers to 14 and 15 July only, not the whole summer. The speaker is the Greater Tunis director; the article does not mention Kerker. The restart-time points are our paraphrase of the page text.
