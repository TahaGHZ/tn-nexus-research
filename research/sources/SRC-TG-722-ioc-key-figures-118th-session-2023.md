Columns are Production, Imports, Consumption, Exports, in 1,000 tonnes (p. 1).

English gloss: in November 2023 the IOC put Tunisian production at 240 kt (2021/22, final), 180 kt (2022/23, provisional) and 200 kt (2023/24, estimate).

## Notes

The 2022/23 provisional figure (180 kt) was revised to 217 kt in the final balance (SRC-TG-723). Both values are recorded because the drought-year figure widely quoted in the press is 180 kt.
