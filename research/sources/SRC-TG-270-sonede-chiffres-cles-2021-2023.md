English gloss: SONEDE's specific energy consumption was 0.685 kWh/m3 in 2022 and 0.725 kWh/m3 in
2023. Volume produced was 806.4 million m3 in 2022 and 773.3 million m3 in 2023; volume distributed
683.7 and 667.9 million m3; volume consumed (billed) 522.3 and 516.0 million m3.

## Notes

The page does not define the denominator of "consommation spécifique énergie" (per m3 produced,
distributed or consumed) nor what it includes (production, transfer pumping, distribution). Any
total GWh derived from it is our own arithmetic and depends on that choice. The linked 2023
statistics synthesis returned HTTP 403 ("Accès refusé") when fetched on 2026-11-21, so the
underlying report could not be opened. The `year` field is an estimate: the page carries no date,
and its latest data year is 2023.
