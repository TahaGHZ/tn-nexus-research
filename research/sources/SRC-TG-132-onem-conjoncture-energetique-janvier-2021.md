## Extracts

PDF p. 17 (printed "Page17"), "Il convient de noter":

> Concessions Douleb/Semama/Tamesmida: Arrêt total de la production suite à l'invasion du site de la production par des manifestants à partir du 11/12/2020

English gloss: total production stop at the Douleb, Semama and Tamesmida concessions after
protesters occupied the production site from 11 December 2020.

Same page, chart "Production mensuelle de pétrole brut (kt)" (January 2010 to January 2021), annotation boxes read from the rendered page image:

> Arrêt de la production dans les champs de sud en 2017

> Fermeture de la vanne d'El Kamour à du 16/07 au 05/11/2020

English gloss: the official monthly crude production chart marks two dips: the production stop
in the southern fields in 2017, and the closure of the El Kamour valve from 16 July to
5 November 2020.

Same page, text:

> La moyenne journalière de la production de pétrole est passée de 35.1 mille barils/j en janvier 2020 à 41.4 mille barils/j en janvier 2021.

English gloss: average daily oil production was 35,100 barrels/day in January 2020 and 41,400
barrels/day in January 2021.

## Notes

The chart's annotations are the only place in the ONEM bulletins opened where the El Kamour
blockade is named; the bulletin does not quantify the lost output. The chart curve itself
(about 330 kt per month in early 2010, falling below 200 kt by the mid-2010s) was not used for
figures because values can only be read approximately.
