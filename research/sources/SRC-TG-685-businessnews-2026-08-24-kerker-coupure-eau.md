## Extracts

Article of Monday 24 August 2026, 11:19.

> Une coupure d'électricité a provoqué, lundi 24 août 2026, l'arrêt de la station principale de pompage de Kerker, qui approvisionne plusieurs zones des gouvernorats de Sfax et de Mahdia.

> annonce des perturbations et des coupures dans l'alimentation en eau potable à partir de 10 heures et jusqu'à 20 heures.

> Dans le gouvernorat de Mahdia, les perturbations concernent les délégations d'El Jem, Boumerdes, Sidi Alouane, Melloulech, Bradaa, Chebba, Souassi, Ouled Chamekh, Hebira et Chorbane.

English gloss: a power cut stopped the main Kerker pumping station on Monday 24 August 2026; it supplies several areas of Sfax and Mahdia. SONEDE announced disruptions and cuts from 10:00 to 20:00. In Mahdia the delegations of El Jem, Boumerdes, Sidi Alouane, Melloulech, Bradaa, Chebba, Souassi, Ouled Chamekh, Hebira and Chorbane are concerned.

## Notes

Press report relaying a SONEDE notice. The extracts give the cause as "une coupure d'électricité" and do not say why the power was cut (load shedding, local fault or heat damage), nor link it to load shedding. The 10:00 to 20:00 window is the announced disruption, not a measured outage. Extracts are as returned by the page text and may be fragments of longer sentences.
