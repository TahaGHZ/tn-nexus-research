## Extracts

> "A decree to establish a certification system for companies and personnel of the RAC servicing sector."

> "In total, 105 trainers (16 women among them) have participated in these sessions and 76 of them have received the corresponding certification. Secondly, between 2020 and 2024, 386 technicians have received training on the good practices for managing and handling refrigerants"

> "For the air-conditioning units, the local air-conditioning manufacturers have completely replaced the use of HCFC-22 by R-410A and this substance should be replaced by HFC-32 according to the recently approved Kigali Implementation Plan (KIP)."

> "The air-conditioning systems are currently based on R-410A and HCFC-22, but HFC-32 is expected to become predominant by 2045."

> "Training and certification sessions for 200 RAC technicians. To be implemented." (planned-activities table)

## Notes

A funding request, so the figures are the implementing agency's own reporting of training
delivered. It covers the whole refrigeration and air-conditioning (RAC) servicing sector and
does not mention VRV or VRF systems or technicians trained on them. The PDF's date is
November 2024 (file metadata).
