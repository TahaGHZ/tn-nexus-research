## Notes

Official ministry observatory, compiling OEP (herd, yields, prices), DG/PA (production) and INS/customs (trade). Chart values on pp. 4, 5 and 7 are read from data labels; the year assigned to each label is inferred from label order in the extracted text, so year-by-year values (except 2024/2025, which also appear in the p. 1 table) should be treated as "read from chart". The production series is consistent with the table: 1,230 thousand t in 2025 and a 2016-2025 TCAM of -1.5% (1408 to 1230 gives -1.5%/yr). Ratios check: 29.4/1230 = 2.4% (printed 2.3%), 58.7/292.1 = 20%. Herd numbers after 2019 are estimates, since no census has been run since 2019. The milk price revision date (November 2022) differs from the ministry's statement to parliament reported in SRC-TG-766 (February 2023); the July 2020 value here (1,040) also differs from the ministry's "945 millimes in August 2020".
