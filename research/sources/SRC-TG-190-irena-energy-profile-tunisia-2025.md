## Notes

Text extracted with pdftotext from the downloaded PDF. The layout mode scrambles labels, so
rows were taken from the raw text order and checked for internal consistency (components add to
totals). IRENA combines official returns and its own estimates.

Disagreements with other sources:
- Solar generation of 914 GWh in 2023 is above ONEM's 2024 national solar output of about
  631 GWh (SRC-TG-193). The source does not explain the gap. A likely reason is that IRENA counts
  self-produced rooftop output, which ONEM counts only from January 2024 (SRC-TG-100
  methodological note).
- Renewable capacity of 1,086 MW (end 2024) is far above the World Bank figure of 516 MW at end
  2023 (SRC-TG-105). They probably differ on whether rooftop and self-production capacity is
  included.
