## Notes

- Official daily bulletin of the dams directorate; ONAGRI republishes it. Figures are provisional and the next bulletins revise some of them (e.g. the previous-year inflow and stock columns do not always match the figure printed a year earlier).
- Column order changed between bulletin versions (2015-2022 vs 2023-2025 layout); read each row against its own header. Rows were taken from `-raw` text because `-layout` mis-aligned dam labels with values.
- Page 1 is an Arabic summary that did not extract as text (font encoding), so it was not used.
- Opened via: curl+pdftotext; saw: partial (French tables on pages 2-3 in full; Arabic page 1 not readable).
