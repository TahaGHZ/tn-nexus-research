## Extracts

PDF p. 5, "Le volet de l'exploitation gazière":

> Le fonctionnement des centrales électriques à environ 97% au gaz naturel.

English gloss: power plants ran about 97% on natural gas in 2016.

PDF p. 8, table of STEG electricity production by fuel (columns 2015 GWh, share, 2016 GWh, share, change):

> Gaz naturel | 13458,6 | 90,6% | 14286,0 | 96,5% | 6%
> Fuel lourd | 873,1 | 5,9% | 0,4 | 0,0% | -100%
> Eolienne | 448,4 | 3,0% | 474,3 | 3,2% | 6%
> Total | 14850,6 | 100% | 14805,9 | 100% | 0%

> Le gaz naturel continue d'occuper une place prépondérante dans la part des combustibles utilisés pour la production électrique STEG avec un taux de près de 97%.

English gloss: in STEG's own production, natural gas was 90.6% in 2015 (heavy fuel oil 5.9%) and
96.5% in 2016 (heavy fuel oil nearly zero). Gas stays dominant at nearly 97% of STEG fuels.

## Notes

Activity report of STEG's gas department, published by the state utility. Classed as company
material (tier C) under the repo rule, but it is the primary record of STEG's own operations. It
covers STEG plants only, not the Radès IPP. The 2015 fuel-oil share matches the dip in the WDI
gas share for 2015 (SRC-TG-102). Text taken from the PDF text layer; the publication year is
assumed to be 2017 because the report covers 2016.
