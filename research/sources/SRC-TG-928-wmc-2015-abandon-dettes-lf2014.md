## Extracts

Article of 22 January 2015, quoting the Minister of Agriculture Lassaad Lachaal (Béja, 21 January 2015):

> plus de 80.000 agriculteurs pour un montant dépassant 115 millions de dinars

English gloss: more than 80,000 farmers, for an amount above TND 115 million.

On the legal basis (loi de finances 2014):

> l'Etat renonce au principal et aux intérêts au titre des crédits agricoles obtenus à la fin de décembre 2012 [...] dont le montant dépasse (principal) 5.000 dinars

English gloss: the state waives principal and interest on agricultural loans obtained up to end December 2012 "whose amount exceeds (principal) TND 5,000".

## Notes

Tier C. The threshold wording ("dépasse", exceeds) is doubtful: search-result summaries of 2013 coverage of the draft law (not opened, so not a source) describe a ceiling of TND 5,000, and the 1999 precedent (SRC-TG-927) also used a ceiling. A write-off of loans above a threshold would be unusual and may be a reporting error. The official text of Loi n° 2013-54 (Finance Law 2014) was not opened; the threshold is therefore unconfirmed.
