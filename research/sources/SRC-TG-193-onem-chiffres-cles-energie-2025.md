## Extracts

p. 17, "Approvisionnement : électricité", text:

> Augmentation progressive de la part des énergies renouvelables dans le parc de production centralisé avec l'entrée en production de 124 MW en 2025 des projets solaires dans le cadre du régime des autorisations et des concessions.

English gloss: the renewable share of the centralised fleet is rising gradually; 124 MW of solar
projects under the authorisation and concession regimes started producing in 2025.

p. 17, chart "Parc de production électrique net (hors autoproduction) 2024-2025" (MW, labels read from the rendered page):

> 2024 (P= 6012MW): ER 339 | Turbines à gaz 2484 | Cycles combinés 2 528 | Thermique à vapeur 660
> 2025 (P= 6106MW): ER 463 | Turbines à gaz 2484 | Cycles combinés 2528 | Thermique à vapeur 660

English gloss: excluding self-production, renewable capacity in the net generating fleet was
339 MW in 2024 and 463 MW in 2025. The pie chart beside it shows renewables at 8% of the
STEG + IPP fleet in 2025.

p. 18, table "Production d'électricité *" (unit GWh; 2024 / 2025 / Var %; * hors autoproducteurs thermiques):

> FUEL + GASOIL 72 / 18 / -75%

English gloss: STEG wind output fell from 337 GWh (2024) to 259 GWh (2025), -23%. Private solar

20,312 GWh in 2025.

p. 18, pie charts "Mix de la production électrique" (y compris autoproduction PV):

> 2025: GAZ NATUREL 94,6% | HYDRAULIQUE 0,1% | EOLIENNE 1,3% | SOLAIRE 3,9%

English gloss: including rooftop self-production, renewables (hydro + wind + solar) were 5.2% of
generation in 2024 and 5.3% in 2025; gas was about 94.5%.

p. 19, table "Electricité" (GWh; 2024 / 2025 / Var %):

## Notes

Published by ONEM in 2026 with 2025 data. Pages 39-41 are images with no text layer; they were
transcribed from the PDF pages rendered at 200 dpi. Pages 17-19 have a text layer; table rows
were taken from the raw extraction and checked by addition (STEG components sum to the STEG
total in both years).

Internal and external inconsistencies:
- The production table excludes thermal self-producers and gives a 2025 total of 20,312 GWh.
  The September 2026 bulletin (SRC-TG-100) gives a 2025 national production of 20,533 GWh and
  IPP solar of 81 GWh (here 37 GWh). The total gap equals third-party purchases (221 GWh).
- The p. 39 table says Sidi Bouzid entered service in January 2026, before the end-2025 cut-off
  of the table title; the red highlight suggests a late update.
