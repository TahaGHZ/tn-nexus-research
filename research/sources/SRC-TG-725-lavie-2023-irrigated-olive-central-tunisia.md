## Notes

- Qualitative study based on 18 interviews (2019) in the Sfax and Sidi Bouzid governorates, plus diachronic photo-interpretation (2002-2020) of two wadi catchments. Local, not national, evidence on irrigation and groundwater.
- The 80,000 to 100,000 t irrigated-oil target is the ONH CEO's statement relayed by the authors (see also SRC-TG-730).
- The 7.4% bottled share (2020, ONH bulletin) differs from the 11% in FAO 2015 (SRC-TG-726) and the 15% in Trésor/ONAGRI (SRC-TG-724).
