## Notes

Monthly provisional data. The PDF has a text layer, but the text extraction scrambles the column
headers, so every table row above was checked against the rendered page image. The internal
figures do not fully agree. Generation is "about 91%" gas on p. 28 and 91.3% on p. 34. The
royalty share is 22% of supply on p. 24, but 531 of 3,979 ktep-pci is about 13%. The gap is
probably the 444 million m3 overdraw noted on p. 22, but the source does not say so. The mix on
p. 34 includes renewable self-production, which is counted from January 2024. Earlier series
excluded it, so shares before and after 2024 are not strictly comparable.
