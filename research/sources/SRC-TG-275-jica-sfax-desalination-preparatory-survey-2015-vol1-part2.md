## Extracts

Printed p. 5-1, Table 5.1-1 "Required Energy for Seawater Desalination by Process" (source given as
"p355, Suido Kogaku, Kenji Fujita, et al, 2006"; columns: necessary power kWh/m3, necessary
calories kcal/m3):

> RO (recovery: 30%) | 4.7 | 4,020
> RO (recovery: 40%) | 3.5 | 3,010

Printed p. 7-1, section 7.1 "Necessary Power Supply":

> The power consumption at a desalination plant with the installation of the RO membrane method with energy recovery system is generally 4 kWh/m3. The Djerba sea water desalination plant is designed at a power consumption of less than 4.2 kwh/m3 according to the bidding document of the plant construction project.

> 4.2 kWh/m3 x 8,333 m3/h = 35,000 kW = 35 MW

> The required power is 38.1 MW. For the plan, 40 MW is applied for the design.

Printed p. 7-2 and 7-3, section 7.2.2:

> In case the Djerba Sea Water Desalination Plant, which was contracted in September 2014 for a capacity of 50,000 m3/d, and the Sfax Seawater Desalination Plant with a capacity of 100,000 m3/d for Phase 1, are operated at the same time, the power consumption by both plants will be 192GWh/year assuming 3.5 kWh/m3 (refer to Table 5.1-1). This will increase the share of the Water Supply and Sewerage Sector in the national total power sales from 3.9% (Figure 2.2-8) to 5.3% on the basis of 2013 values. Power consumption by both plants will account for 1.3% of national power sales.

> As an emergency case, if power supply is in danger at the peak power demand hours, operation of the desalination process can be tentatively suspended for a few hours because the storage capacity of produced water for 6 hours [...] will be provided for the plant

Printed p. 7-9, section 7.4 "Electricity Tariff":

> The basic tariff will be 10.5 TND/kW/month for receiving two power lines. Since the tariff is varied on a seasonal basis, the annual average tariff is 0.148 TND/kWh (Table 7.4-1).

Same page, on on-site solar:

> it will take almost 18 years to recover initial investment cost

## Notes

The 35 MW figure is for the full 200,000 m3/day final stage assumed in 2015 (SONEDE now gives
250,000 m3/day, SRC-TG-273). The 4.2 kWh/m3 value is a design ceiling from the Djerba tender and
3.5 kWh/m3 is a textbook value for 40% recovery; neither is a measured operating figure. The
survey advised against on-site solar for the plant on 2015 module costs (300,000 yen/kW), which are
now outdated. The tariff is the 2015 high-voltage tariff.
