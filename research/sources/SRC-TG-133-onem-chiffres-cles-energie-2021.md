## Notes

Classic monthly-bulletin approach (no biomass, royalty shown separately). The text extraction
places the demand sub-rows out of line, but the resource, demand and balance rows above are
consistent: 5,094 - 9,714 = -4,620 (printed -4,619). Computed from this table, the 2021 deficit
without royalty is 5,598 / 9,714 = 58% of demand, close to the "environ 57% (hors redevance)"
in the 2035 strategy synthesis (SRC-TG-103).
