Columns are Production, Imports, Consumption, Exports, in 1,000 tonnes (p. 1).

English gloss: the final 2022/23 figure is 217 kt production and 198.5 kt exports. The 2024/25 estimate made in November 2024 was 340 kt production and 310 kt exports.

## Notes

The 2024/25 export estimate here (310 kt) was later revised to 275 kt (SRC-TG-721).
