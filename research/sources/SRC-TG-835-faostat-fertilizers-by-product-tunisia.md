## Notes

Production stops after 2012 and agricultural use after 2016 for these products, so FAOSTAT cannot show recent domestic supply. Trade quantities match UN Comtrade (SRC-TG-824) and probably derive from it. Recorded AN imports for 2020 and 2021 (7,255 t and 8,100 t) are far below the 60,000 t import via GCT that FAO reports for 2020 (SRC-TG-820); SRC-TG-837 dates the 60,000 t to three lots in December 2020 and January 2021, but recorded 2020 + 2021 imports total only about 15,000 t, so the gap remains unresolved (customs classification or reporting error are possible explanations).
