## Extracts

Article 21, JORT pp. 2826–2827, amends the procedure applicable to renewable electricity projects on agricultural land and the allocation of grid connection costs. Consult the reproduced official text for the conditions of the State-site exception. The legal regime does not by itself measure the length or causes of project delays.
