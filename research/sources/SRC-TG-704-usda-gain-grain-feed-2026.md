Production policy:

> Tunisia maintained MY 2025/26 farm-gate prices for domestic wheat and barley as follows: Durum Wheat: 1,300 TD/MT ($438/MT), Common Wheat: 1,000 TD/MT ($337/MT), Barley: 800 TD/MT ($270/MT).

> Subsidizing irrigation water $0.07/m3 (32 percent of the cost) to encourage increasing irrigated wheat area to 120,000 HA,

Consumption:

> Per capita consumption is estimated at around 241 kg in MY 2026/27, with a population of 12.4 million.

Trade policy:

> Tunisia's Cereal Board controls imports and exports of wheat and wheat products. As the monopoly importer, all Cereal Board wheat tenders are destined for domestic use. Imported wheat is sold on the market at a subsidized rate, and the difference is covered by the Tunisian government.

> Unlike wheat, barley imports are liberalized and private sector companies are allowed to import directly instead of going through Tunisia's Office des Cereales (Cereal Board).
