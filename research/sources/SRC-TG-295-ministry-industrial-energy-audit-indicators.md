## Extracts

Official ministry table, “Secteur Industriel,” row “Nb d’audits” (columns 2010-2019):

> 2010 101 | 2011 103 | 2012 50 | 2013 38 | 2014 38 | 2015 44 | 2016 54 | 2017 29 | 2018 21 | 2019 -

English gloss: the ministry reports annual counts of industrial audits, from 103 in 2011 to 21
in 2018; the 2019 value is not reported.

## Notes

Official Ministry webpage, opened in its HTML text layer on 2026-11-21. It does not define
whether counts are completed audits, newly audited establishments, or repeat audits; it gives no
eligible-establishment denominator or compliance rate. The series establishes a decline in the
reported count, not weak enforcement by itself.
