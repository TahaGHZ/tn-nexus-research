## Extracts

> n'ont pas dépassé les 25,5 millions de dinars (MDT)

English gloss: agricultural receivables written off by BNA on its own funds (first half 2023 accounts) did not exceed TND 25.5 million.

> Il s'agit de la loi n° 65-99 du 15 juillet 1999 et de la loi n° 2013-54, relative à la loi des finances de l'année 2014.

English gloss: the write-offs were made under Law 99-65 of 15 July 1999 and Law 2013-54 (Finance Law 2014).

## Notes

Tier C, wire story of 14 September 2023. Used only to establish the legal basis (Law 99-65 and Law 2013-54) of BNA agricultural write-offs. The article's figures on BNA's total receivables were not used because their scope is unclear in the text.
