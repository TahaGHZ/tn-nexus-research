## Notes

Fresh tomatoes and processed tomato products (paste, peeled) are separate items; tomato paste is reported here in product weight, with no fresh-equivalent conversion. FAOSTAT trade is reported by calendar year, while Tunisian date and citrus campaigns run across years, so values differ from ONAGRI campaign figures.
