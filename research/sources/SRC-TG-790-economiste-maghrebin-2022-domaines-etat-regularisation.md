## Extracts

Article of 6 July 2022, reporting Mohamed Rekik, Minister of State Domains and Land Affairs:

> près de 500 mille hectares de terres domaniales sont exploitées

> par plus de 50 mille exploitants sans que leurs situations ne soient régularisées

English gloss: about 500,000 ha of state land are farmed by more than 50,000 operators whose situation has not been regularised.

## Notes

Press report (TAP wire) of a ministerial statement; fragments obtained through a fetch tool. The wording leaves it ambiguous whether all 500,000 ha are farmed by unregularised operators or whether the 50,000 unregularised operators farm part of it. Treat as an order of magnitude.
