## Extracts

Abstract fragments (University of Malaga repository record, opened):

> Spain carried out an intense irrigation modernization process

> Nevertheless, certain unfavourable effects of irrigation modernization also need to be discussed.

English gloss: Spain's 2002-2015 modernisation aimed at water savings, flexibility and supply guarantees; the authors review unfavourable effects using a DPSIR framework and the empirical literature.

Web-search summary of another copy of the abstract (core.ac.uk, which returned 403 when opened; not a verified quote): the consumption rebound effect "has been heterogeneous", attributed to limits on area expansion, water-allocation cutbacks and existing deficit irrigation; increased energy use is flagged as a negative outcome.

## Notes

- The key result (heterogeneous rebound, higher energy use) rests on a search summary, not on text I read. Do not cite figures from this paper. The full text is open access and should be read before the record leaves draft.
- Spain differs from Tunisia in having basin authorities with legal allocations and area limits, which the summary names as what limited the rebound.
