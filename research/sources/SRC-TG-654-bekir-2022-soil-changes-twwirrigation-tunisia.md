## Extracts

Abstract (DOI 10.3390/agriculture12122139, 13 December 2022):

> Two perimeters (P1 and P2) receiving TWW for 38 and 20 years, respectively, in Tunisia were selected for study. In each perimeter, two water types were adopted: TWW and groundwater (GW).

> compared to GW, TWW enhanced the soil salinity by 127%, particularly at deeper subsoils.

> TWW showed the lowest CROSS and SAR indexes, indicating the benefits of applying TWW even after long-term use in improving soil physicochemical parameters such as sodicity and structural stability.
