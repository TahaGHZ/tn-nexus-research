## Extracts

Article by Mohsen Tiss, 12 January 2026, reporting an Assembly of Representatives study day and quoting BCT official Sofiane Ben Nour:

> le nombre d’acteurs du secteur agricole classés en catégories 4 et 5 (à haut risque) auprès des banques publiques s’élevait, à la fin du mois de juin 2025, à 33 535, dont 4 785 entreprises et 28 750 personnes physiques

English gloss: at end-June 2025, 33,535 agricultural sector actors were classified in categories 4 and 5 at public banks, including 4,785 firms and 28,750 individuals.

> l’encours a atteint environ 4 600 millions de dinars à la fin novembre 2025

English gloss: outstanding agricultural credit reached about TND 4,600 million at end-November 2025.

## Notes

Tier C press report quoting named officials and attributing the data to an Assembly study day. The 33,535 figure covers high-risk actors at public banks, not all farmers or all banks. The outstanding credit figure is a different measure from overdue or classified debt.
