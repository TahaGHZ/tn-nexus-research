## Extracts

Article 5 sets the timing of the commission’s work:

> la commission se réunit périodiquement tous les trois mois

The same article uses three rolling months of international prices for petrol and the two specified grades of diesel. This is a joint ministerial order implementing quarterly review; the January 2014 programme benchmark and any subsequent suspension are separate questions.
