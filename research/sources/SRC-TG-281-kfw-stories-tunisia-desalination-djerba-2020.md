## Extracts

> According to the head of the national water distribution utility, they are the largest energy customer of the state's electricity supplier, STEG.

> But Helali says, "our plant has the lowest energy consumption of comparable plants around the world with 2.5 kilowatt hours per 1,000 litres of drinking water."

> Published on KfW Stories: 16 March 2020.

English gloss: SONEDE's head describes the utility as STEG's largest energy customer. The Djerba
plant manager claims 2.5 kWh per m3.

## Notes

Lender's promotional story (by Christoph Albrecht-Heider) quoting the plant manager. The 2.5
kWh/m3 is an operator claim with no stated boundary (membranes only, or including intake,
pre-treatment and transfer pumping). It is lower than the 4.2 kWh/m3 design ceiling in the Djerba
tender reported by JICA (SRC-TG-275) and the 3 kWh/m3 average SONEDE gave in 2021 (SRC-TG-279).
