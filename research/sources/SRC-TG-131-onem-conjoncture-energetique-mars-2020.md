English gloss: in full-year 2019, 6 permits were granted, 6 exploration wells drilled and 4

> Le nombre total de permis en cours de validité à fin mars 2020, estde25 dont 17permis de recherche et 8 permis de prospection, couvrant une superficie totale de 86 451 km2. Le nombre total de concessions est de 56dont 42en production.

English gloss: at end-March 2020 there were 25 valid permits (17 exploration/research permits
and 8 prospecting permits) covering 86,451 km2, and 56 concessions, 42 of them producing.
