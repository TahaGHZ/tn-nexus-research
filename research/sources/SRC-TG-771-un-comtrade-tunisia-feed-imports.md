## Notes

Tunisia-reported customs data in calendar years, so they are not comparable one-for-one with USDA marketing years (SRC-TG-763, SRC-TG-764). The 2024 query was re-run on 2026-11-21. The 2021 and 2023 responses were saved by the source-verifier run and read here. The 2024 soybean volume (343 kt) is well below USDA's MY 2024/25 estimate (507 kt). Barley in 2023 (1.43 Mt) is higher than any single USDA marketing year (774 kt in MY 2023/24).
