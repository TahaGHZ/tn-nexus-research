## Extracts

PDF pp. 51–52, proposed subsidy expenditure. Short extracts transcribed from the Arabic text:

> تم تخصيص مبلغ 11846 م.د لنفقات الدعم

> وتخصيص 7090 م.د

English gloss: the proposal allocates 11,846 million TND to subsidies in total, including a 7,090 million TND hydrocarbon subsidy appropriation shared between STIR and STEG. The cited passage gives no allocation by utility. These are budget proposals, not cash payments.

PDF p. 51, oil-price assumption: Brent at USD 80/bbl. PDF p. 52, sensitivity estimates: 172 million TND per USD 1/bbl increase and 59 million TND per 10-millime increase in the dollar exchange rate. The document also describes 600 million TND of proposed STEG performance measures and approximately 820 million TND in combined arrears settlement for STIR and STEG. The combined arrears figure is not specific to STEG.
