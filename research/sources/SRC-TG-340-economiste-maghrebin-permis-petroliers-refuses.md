## Extracts

Article, 10 July 2014:

> Il s'agit des permis «Borj El Khadra», «Baguel» (Douz) et «El Franig» (Médenine).

English gloss: the permits concerned are Borj El Khadra, Baguel (Douz) and El Franig (Médenine).

> En raison des infractions légales et financières, l'adoption des trois projets de loi

English gloss: because of legal and financial infractions, the adoption of the three draft laws (on extending the research permits) was rejected by the energy committee of the National Constituent Assembly. The article gives different reasons by permit: infractions over a 23-year period for Borj El Khadra; for Baguel and El Franig, the operator Perenco invoked the 1985 decree's 30-year cap, so its 15-year extension request was not admissible.
