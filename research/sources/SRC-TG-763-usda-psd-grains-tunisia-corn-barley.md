## Notes

USDA estimates (official USDA series, informed by Tunisian ministry data and the FAS Tunis post), not Tunisian national statistics. Market years: the barley MY for Tunisia starts in July (GAIN, SRC-TG-704), so "2023" = July 2023 to June 2024; the corn MY start month was not confirmed. Market years do not match the calendar years used by INS and ITES. The values are revised over time: the April 2026 GAIN report (SRC-TG-704, Table 5) shows "USDA Official" barley MY 2024/25 imports of 620 and feed use of 800 thousand t, later revised to 519 and 700 in this file (update of May 2026). Feed use of barley includes small ruminants on rangeland, not only intensive units.
