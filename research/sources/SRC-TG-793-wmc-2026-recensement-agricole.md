## Extracts

Article of 14 October 2026:

> La Tunisie projette de réaliser le premier, recensement général agricole numérique 2026-2027, de son histoire

> la saison agricole 2025-2026 représente une saison référentielle pour le recensement

English gloss: Tunisia plans the first general agricultural census in its history in 2026-2027; the 2025-2026 season is the reference season. Field work takes place in the following season and is to finish before the 2027-2028 season (paraphrase of the fetch result).

## Notes

Press report. Used only to show that no census results exist yet, so the 2004-05 structure survey (SRC-TG-780) remains the latest published farm-structure data. Farm-number estimates quoted elsewhere in the press (about 830,000; "nearly one million") were not opened and are not used.
