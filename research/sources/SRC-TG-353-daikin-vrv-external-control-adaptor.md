## Notes

Manufacturer technical documentation (tier C). It confirms that an external contact closure on the
adaptor makes the outdoor unit limit its demand, with guideline levels of about 70% (Demand 1) and
about 40% (Demand 2), and a forced thermo-off (Demand 3). It does not state an 80% level, the
quantity the percentages refer to (it reads as a share of capacity or input), or any timer
wiring; the use of a 13:00-17:00 timer is the use case in SRC-TG-341, not this manual. The PDF is a
North American copy of Daikin engineering data; the Tunisian product range may differ. It gives
no measured saving.
