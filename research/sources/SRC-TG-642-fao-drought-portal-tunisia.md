## Extracts

> "Rainfall varies geographically, with around 600 mm in the north, 300 mm in the center, 150 mm in the south, and less than 100 mm in the extreme southwest" (Tunisia page, climate paragraph)

> "Annual evapotranspiration is about 1200 mm in the north and more than 180.0 mm in the south." (same page; the southern figure is a typo in the source, probably 1800 mm)

> "Recurrent long droughts are experienced in the country, often lasting more than a year." (same page)

English gloss: rainfall is about 600 mm (north), 300 mm (centre), 150 mm (south); evapotranspiration is about 1,200 mm in the north; long droughts recur.

## Notes

The year is the portal page's approximate date and was not shown on the page. The page gives long-term averages by region; it says nothing on the number or intensity of rain events, which is the figure that matters for storage sizing. The south figure for evapotranspiration is garbled and is not used.
