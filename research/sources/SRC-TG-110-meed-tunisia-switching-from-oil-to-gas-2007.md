## Extracts

Article dated 12 October 2007. Phrases from the article body:

> all oil-fired power stations due to be converted to natural gas

> all future thermal power plants to be gas-fired

> Tunisia producing almost no gas at all before the offshore Miskar field

> the offshore Miskar field was first developed in the 1990s

> Miskar, which accounts for almost 80 per cent of total Tunisian gas production.

> power demand is growing at about 7 per cent a year

> its ageing fields have declined in production over the past 15 years

English gloss: in 2007 STEG planned to convert all oil-fired power stations to gas, and all future
thermal plants were to be gas-fired. Tunisia produced almost no gas before Miskar, which was
developed in the 1990s and supplied almost 80% of Tunisian gas output in 2007. Power demand was
growing about 7% a year. Oil fields had been declining for 15 years.
