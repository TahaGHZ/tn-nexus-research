## Extracts

p. 18, "II. Données nationales", chart "Ressources et consommation d'énergie primaire" (ktep, 1990-2013, "Source : ONE"), read from the rendered page image:

> Excédent énergétique | Equilibre | Déficit énergétique

> 2013 labels: 8757 (Consommation) | 6225 (Ressources)

English gloss: the chart's header bands mark an energy surplus from 1990 to 1999, "equilibrium"
at 2000 (dashed line), and an energy deficit from 2001 onwards. The 2013 labels are 8,757 ktep
consumption and 6,225 ktep resources.

Same page, chart "Solde énergétique" (ktep, 1990-2013):

> Excédent énergétique (shaded 1990-2000) | Déficit énergétique (shaded 2001-2013)

English gloss: the energy balance is shown positive up to 2000 and negative from 2001. Read
approximately from the curve, it stayed between about -200 and -1,000 ktep over 2001-2010, then
fell to about -2,500 ktep in 2013. Only the labelled values are used as figures.

p. 28, "III-2 Efficacité énergétique", chart "Nombre de contrats programmes" (cumulative number, 1990-2013, "Source : ANME"), read from the rendered page image (added for FND-TG-291):

> 1 183 | 64% | 8% | 28% ; légende : Tertiaire | Transport | Industrie

English gloss: the cumulative number of energy-efficiency programme contracts reached 1,183 in
2013. The 2013 bar is labelled 64% (industry, top segment), 8% (transport) and 28% (tertiary,
bottom segment). Read by eye, the cumulative total was under about 300 in 2005, rose to about
940 in 2010, and grew more slowly to 1,183 by 2013; only the printed labels are used as figures.

p. 29, chart "Cogénération : puissance installée et électricité produite" (2002-2013, "Source : ANME"), printed labels:

> Capacité installée (cumulée) (MW): 2002: 5 | 2007: 6,5 | 2008: 16,5 | 2009: 26,5 | 2010: 32,6 | 2011: 36,6 | 2012: 36,6 | 2013: 54,1
> Production d'électricité (GWh): 2002: 40 | 2007: 48 | 2008: 101 | 2009: 164 | 2010: 202 | 2011: 236 | 2012: 236 | 2013: 350

English gloss: cumulative cogeneration capacity was 54.1 MW in 2013, producing 350 GWh.

p. 29, chart "Economies d'énergie provenant des actions d'efficacité énergétique" (ktep, 2008-2013), 2013 bar labels:

> Contrats programmes 31% | Cogénération 6% | LBC 53%

English gloss: in 2013, programme contracts provided 31% of the energy savings attributed to
efficiency actions, cogeneration 6%, and low-consumption lamps 53%.

## Notes

Brochure by the energy efficiency agency, using ONE (now ONEM) data. The resource series
appears to follow the classic approach (royalty included), but the brochure does not state the
method on this page. Chart values other than the printed labels are read by eye and are not used
as indicators. The text layer does not carry the chart values.
