## Notes

- Provisional figures. All four subsidy lines at end-December 2025 equal the LF 2025
  appropriation to the decimal, while end-November 2025 was only 5,967.6 MDT. This suggests the
  year-end figure books the appropriation rather than a measured outturn, or that subsidies are
  regularised in one block in December. Treat 2025 as provisional.
- "Carburants" covers both the refinery company STIR (fuel products) and STEG (electricity and
  gas). The split is not given in this document; SRC-TG-161 gives it for 2024 (STIR 3,067, STEG
  4,019 MDT).
- Column headers for 2023 and 2022 are year totals as updated by the ministry (note 5).
- The PDF text layer is scrambled; values were read from the rendered page image and checked
  against word coordinates.
