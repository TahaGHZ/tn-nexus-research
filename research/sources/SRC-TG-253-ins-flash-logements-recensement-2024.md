## Extracts

p. 1, section 1:

> Selon le recensement de 2024, la Tunisie comptait, 4265636 dont 80.7% logements habités et 19.3% logements vacants.

> Au cours de la dernière décennie (2014-2024), il s'est accru de 975.7 mille unités par rapport à 2014, où l'on comptait 3 289,9 mille logements, soit une augmentation annuelle moyenne nette de 97.6 mille logements contre 78.9 mille entre (2004-2014) [...]

English gloss: 4,265,636 dwellings in 2024; net increase of 975,700 since 2014, an average net
increase of 97,600 dwellings a year (78,900 a year in 2004-2014).

p. 2, Tableau 3 "Type de logements par milieu" (national, thousands and %):

> HOUCH/ DAR ARBI/ BORJ 677.7 (15.9) | LOGEMENT JUMELÉ OU ÉTAGE LOGEMENT JUMELÉ 2093.5 (49.1) | VILLA OU DUPLEX/STUDIO 1111.2 (26.1) | APPARTEMENT 360.1 (8.4) | LOGEMENT RUDIMENTAIRE 23.2 (0.5) | NATIONAL 4265.6 (100.0)

> Les appartements restent minoritaires (8,4 %) [...]

English gloss: apartments are 8.4% of dwellings; attached houses or floors of attached houses
49.1%, villas or duplexes 26.1%, traditional houses 15.9%.

## Notes

Dwelling type is not the same as construction mode (self-built by an individual vs built by a
developer). An attached house or a floor of one can be built by a developer, and some
apartments are self-built. The table shows that collective apartment buildings are a small part
of the stock, but it does not measure how many dwellings fall under the exemption for
individual houses built by private persons in the thermal regulation.
