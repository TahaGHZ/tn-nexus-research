## Extracts

Article of 7 November 2026, reporting Wajdi Hedhili, Minister of State Domains and Land Affairs, at a joint meeting of the CNRD and ARP committees:

> Les terres agricoles domaniales couvrent une superficie totale de 500.000 ha

> dont 38 % sont actuellement exploités dans le cadre de contrats de location

> Vingt quatre terres agricoles domaniales, d'une superficie globale de plus de 11.000 hectares (ha)

English gloss: state agricultural land totals 500,000 ha, of which 38% is currently operated under lease contracts; 24 state farms covering more than 11,000 ha have been made available to community companies (sociétés communautaires).
