## Notes

- Opened via: curl of the World Bank API (JSON); saw: full series 1961-2022.
- The numerator is a fixed long-term average of 4.195 km3, so the decline in this series comes entirely from population growth. It does not show any change in the hydrology.
- The figure counts internal renewable resources only. It excludes non-renewable fossil groundwater and non-conventional water, so it is not directly comparable with the MARHP figures of 4.9-5.0 billion m3, which include deep aquifers.
- The series crosses the 500 m3 "absolute scarcity" threshold around 1990-1991.
