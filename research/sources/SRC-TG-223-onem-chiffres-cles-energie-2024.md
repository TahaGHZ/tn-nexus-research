## Extracts

PDF p. 10 (printed p. 19), chart "Evolution annuelle de la pointe électrique (MW)", caption:

> Enregistrement d'un nouveau record de la pointe électrique : 4888 MW le 14 août 2024.

English gloss: a new record electricity peak of 4,888 MW was set on 14 August 2024.

PDF p. 18 (printed p. 33-34), table "Ventes d'électricité par niveau de tension et par région en 2024" (GWh), footnotes:

> (1) hors autoproduction consommée (2) Y compris fraudes et proratas

> Total(1) (2) | 1168 | 7082 | 8840 | 17090

English gloss: electricity sales by voltage level and region in 2024 totalled 17,090 GWh (HV
1,168; MV 7,082; LV 8,840). Self-consumed self-production is excluded. Fraud and pro-rata
estimates are included.

Two-page-per-sheet layout; text was extracted with pdftotext. The regional sales table shows
volumes by STEG district only. It gives no supply-quality or outage figures by region.
