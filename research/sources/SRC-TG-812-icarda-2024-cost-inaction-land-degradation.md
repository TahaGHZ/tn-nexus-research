## Extracts

> "Using APELD, ongoing similar analyses in Tunisia underscore the urgency of addressing land degradation, revealing that inaction to control it costs the country about $2.17 billion (4.65% of GDP) in lost ESS annually."

> "Croplands, irrigation water, and rangelands are the top three sources of loss, accounting for 53.98%, 28.19%, and 12.09% of the total cost of inaction, respectively."

## Notes

News item summarising an analysis then "ongoing". The underlying report (Yigezu et al. 2025, "Costs of Inaction, Benefits of Action, and Returns on Investment to Combat Agricultural Resource Degradation in Tunisia", CGIAR MEL, hdl 20.500.11766/70570) is limited-access and was not read. Scope covers several agricultural resources (the top three sources of loss are cropland, irrigation water and rangeland), so it is broader than soil. Extracts checked by downloading the page (curl). On the same page, a figure of USD 2.83 billion (4.58% of GDP) refers to Uzbekistan, not Tunisia; it has been attributed to Tunisia elsewhere and should not be used. Not the same scope or method as the World Bank 2-7% of GDP figure (SRC-TG-881).
