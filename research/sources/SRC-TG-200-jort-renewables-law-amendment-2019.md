## Extracts

Article 7, replacing article 9 of law 2015-12:

> Il est aussi permis de constituer une société d’autoproduction

A dedicated company can supply eligible self-consumers under the conditions of the amended article, transport their electricity through the national grid, and sell surplus within prescribed limits. This text does not establish a general right to sell to any retail customer.
