## Extracts

> "ACO StormBrixx is a unique and patented plastic geocellular stormwater management system. [...] as a standalone solution or as part of an integrated sustainable urban drainage (SuDS) scheme." (p. 1)

> "Manufactured from recyclable polypropylene" and "50/60 year design life" (p. 1, key benefits)

English gloss: made of recyclable polypropylene; 50 to 60 year design life (manufacturer's statement).

> "High void ratio minimises excavation volume" (p. 1, key benefits)

> "StormBrixx SD (Standard-duty) which has a depth to invert of up to 4.5m" (p. 1)

> "The open cell structure permits completely free access for CCTV and jetting equipment which allows the whole system [...] to be inspected and maintained from just a few access points." (p. 1)

> Technical specification table, SD column: "Void ratio 97%"; "Short term vertical compressive strength 350kN/m²"; "Short term lateral compressive strength 70kN/m²" (p. 2). The PDF text extraction scrambles the table labels and values, so the pairing is read from the table order and matches the 97% / 95% (SD / HD) values given on other ACO country pages.

## Notes

Manufacturer marketing document, tier C, for the New Zealand market. It gives no price, no minimum cover depth, no rule on groundwater level or soil type, no pollutant or water-quality guidance, and no recycled-content or end-of-life statement. The "design life" and "recyclable" statements are the manufacturer's own and are not independently tested here. No Tunisian version or Tunisian installation is documented.
