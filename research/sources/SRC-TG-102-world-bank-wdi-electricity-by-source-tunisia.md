English gloss: in 1990-1993 oil and gas shared Tunisian generation. Gas reached 84.8% in 1995,
stayed between 84% and 90% until 2002, and was about 97% in 2003-2005. Oil fell below 1% in 2003, with spikes in 2006-2009 and 2015. Gas has
stayed between 88.7% and 97.8% since 2003 and was 93.9% in 2024.

## Notes

Human-readable page: https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.NGAS.ZS?locations=TN. The
series starts in 1990, so it cannot show the mix before or right after the Transmed pipeline
opened in 1983. The data are IEA statistics republished by the World Bank, so they are not
independent of IEA. The 1990 and 1991 values look swapped between the two series (gas 63.7 then
35.5, oil 35.5 then 62.7), which suggests a data quality issue in the early years. Only selected years are quoted above; the API returns every year.
