## Extracts

Summary of decree-law 2022-68 as reported (29 October 2022):

> sur des parcelles du domaine public agricole et non agricole

> un droit réel spécial est créé sur les bâtiments, ouvrages et équipements

> ne requiert pas, toutefois, de changement de vocation de ces terres

English gloss: renewable projects may be built on public agricultural and non-agricultural plots under lease; a special real right is created over buildings and equipment but not the land; building on agricultural land does not require a change of use of that land.
