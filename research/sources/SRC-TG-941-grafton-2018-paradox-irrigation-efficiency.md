## Extracts

IWMI catalogue page for the article (opened), quoted fragments of the abstract:

> increased IE rarely delivers the presumed public-good benefits of increased water availability.

> increases in IE must be accompanied by robust water accounting and measurements, a cap on extractions,

English gloss: gains in irrigation efficiency (IE) rarely deliver the water savings that governments assume; they must be paired with water accounting, measurement and a cap on total extractions. Mechanisms named in the search summary of the abstract (not verified quote): more water used by existing crops, a switch to more water-intensive crops, or expansion of irrigated area.
