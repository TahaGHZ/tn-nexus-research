## Extracts

Recital on phase-in:

> To ensure a gradual transition from the current system of free allowances to the CBAM, the CBAM should be progressively phased in while free allowances in sectors covered by the CBAM are phased out.

Recital on declarations:

> The first CBAM declaration, in respect of the calendar year 2026, should be submitted by 31 May 2027.

Article 22(1), surrender of certificates:

> By 31 May of each year, and for the first time in 2027 for the year 2026, the authorised CBAM declarant shall surrender via the CBAM registry a number of CBAM certificates that corresponds to the embedded emissions declared [...] for the calendar year preceding the surrender.

Final article, application:

> Article 2(2) and Articles 4, 6 to 9, 15 and 19, Article 20(1), (3), (4) and (5), Articles 21 to 27 and 31 shall apply from 1 January 2026.

English gloss: CBAM's financial obligations apply from 1 January 2026; certificates for 2026
imports are surrendered for the first time by 31 May 2027; CBAM is phased in as free EU ETS
allowances in covered sectors are phased out.

## Notes

Original 2023 text as served by EUR-Lex at access. Later simplification amendments (2025) may
have changed declaration and surrender dates; the amending act was not opened. The yearly
phase-out schedule of free allocation (Article 31 and the ETS Directive) was not extracted. The
European Commission CBAM page (https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism_en)
states "As of 1 January 2026, CBAM is applicable under its definitive regime" and names the
covered sectors as "cement, iron and steel, aluminium, fertilisers, electricity and hydrogen"
(read through WebFetch).
