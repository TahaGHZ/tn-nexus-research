## Extracts

Abstract (page text opened via curl):

> Desalination of seawater has been chosen in Tunisia as a strategic response to the problem of insufficient water resources. However, this solution may be accompanied by negative effects on the environment. [...] Collected data covers the period from 2015 to 2020. [...] Main results showed positive impacts of the desalination plant with a net increase of drinking water supply and an improvement of the drinking water quality in the island of Djerba. However, the high energy requirements of the desalination plant (13.6% of the total electrical energy consumption in the region) and the important quantity of discharged brine (58 tons/day) rise concerns about negative impacts on the environment.
