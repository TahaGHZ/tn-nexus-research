## Extracts

p. 17, chart "Parc de production électrique net 2019-2020" (MW, labels read from the rendered page):

> 2019 (P= 5653MW): ER 312 | Turbines à gaz 2598 | Cycles combinés 2 083 | Thermique à vapeur 660
> 2020 (P= 5934MW): ER 312 | Turbines à gaz 2879 | Cycles combinés 2083 | Thermique à vapeur 660

English gloss: renewable capacity in the net generating fleet (excluding self-producers) was
312 MW in both 2019 and 2020.

p. 18, table "Production d'électricité *" (GWh; 2019 / 2020 / Var %; * hors autoproducteurs):

> FUEL + GASOIL 0,4 / 19,9 / 4805%
> GAZ NATUREL 16 441 / 15 631 / -5%
> HYDRAULIQUE 66 / 46 / -30%
> EOLIENNE 500 / 465 / -7%
> Solaire PV 0,15 / 0,20 / 33%
> STEG 17 007 / 16 163 / -5,0%
> IPP (GAZ NATUREL) 3 071 / 3 415 / 11%
> TOTAL 20 079 / 19 578 / -2,5%

p. 18, pie charts "Mix de la production électrique":

> 2019: GAZ NATUREL 97,2% | HYDRAULIQUE 0,3% | EOLIENNE 2,5% | SOLAIRE 0,001%
> 2020: GAZ NATUREL 97,4% | HYDRAULIQUE 0,2% | EOLIENNE 2,4% | SOLAIRE 0,001%

English gloss: excluding self-producers, wind produced 500 GWh in 2019 and 465 GWh in 2020.
Renewables (hydro + wind + solar) were about 2.8% of generation in 2019 and 2.6% in 2020.

## Notes

Page numbers are printed page numbers; the table and charts were checked against the rendered
page (PDF page 9). The series excludes all self-producers, including rooftop PV, which ONEM
began counting only in January 2024 (SRC-TG-100). The 2020 share is therefore not directly
comparable with the 2024-2025 shares in SRC-TG-193, which include self-produced PV.
