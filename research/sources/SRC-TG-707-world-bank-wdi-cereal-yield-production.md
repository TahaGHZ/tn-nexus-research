## Notes

The underlying data are FAOSTAT (FAOSTAT itself was unreachable on 2026-11-21, HTTP 521). "Land under cereal production" is harvested area, so in a failed year yield per harvested hectare stays moderate while area and production collapse (2023: area 470,897 ha, production 612,415 t). The 2023 production figure (all cereals) is higher than the GIEWS and USDA estimates (see SRC-TG-700, SRC-TG-703); the mean and CV are our own computation.
