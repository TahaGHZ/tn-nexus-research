## Extracts

> Le taux d'urbanisation a atteint près de 72 % en Tunisie en 2025, tandis que la consommation de terres urbaines est estimée à environ 4 000 hectares par an, dont près de 40 % sont exploités dans le cadre d'une « expansion urbaine anarchique ».

English gloss: the urbanisation rate reached about 72% in 2025, and urban land consumption is estimated at about 4,000 ha per year, nearly 40% of it through "anarchic" (unplanned) urban expansion.

## Notes

Figures presented by Dr Asma El Gharbi (university researcher, territorial development) at a CTFCI round table, as reported on 18 May 2026. The underlying study is not named, and the article does not say how much of the 4,000 ha is farmland.
